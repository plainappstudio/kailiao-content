社区守则 · Community Rules

开聊 · 社交练习室

简体中文

开聊的社区是一个互相学习、真诚分享的地方。发布分享、照片或评论即表示你同意遵守以下守则。

请这样做

  • 尊重每一个人，包括你分享经历中的对方。
  • 分享真实经历与反思，给出友善、具体的建议。
  • 对方说“不”就是“不”。尊重边界是一切交流的前提。

禁止的内容

  • 公开任何他人的照片、聊天截图、姓名、学校、公司、联系方式或其他可识别信息。
  • 照片中出现其他人的脸（包括约会对象）；裸露或性暗示内容。
  • 传授操控、施压、欺骗、跟踪或骚扰他人的手段。
  • 辱骂、歧视、仇恨或贬低任何群体（包括女性）的言论。
  • 广告、引流、诈骗或虚假内容。
  • 任何涉及未成年人的约会或性相关内容。

审核与处理

  • 新分享（含照片）需经人工审核后才会公开。
  • 评论会立即显示；任何人都可以通过「举报」反馈不当内容。
  • 违规内容会被删除；严重或多次违规的账号会被限制发言。

如有疑问或申诉，请联系 plainappsstudio@gmail.com。

English

The KaiLiao community is a place to learn from each other and share honestly. By posting, uploading photos or commenting you agree to these rules.

Do

  • Respect everyone, including the people in the experiences you share.
  • Share real experiences and reflections; give kind, specific advice.
  • "No" means no. Respecting boundaries comes first.

Not allowed

  • Posting anyone else's photos, chat screenshots, name, school, workplace, contact details or other identifying information.
  • Other people's faces in outfit photos; nudity or sexually suggestive content.
  • Teaching manipulation, pressure, deception, stalking or harassment.
  • Abuse, discrimination, hate or degrading any group (including women).
  • Ads, promotion, scams or fake content.
  • Any dating or sexual content involving minors.

Moderation

  • New posts (including photos) are reviewed before they become public.
  • Comments appear immediately; anyone can report inappropriate content.
  • Violating content is removed; accounts with serious or repeated violations are restricted.

Questions or appeals: plainappsstudio@gmail.com.
